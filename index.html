Privacy Policy

Convergence Tracker

Effective Date: February 15, 2026 · Last Updated: February 15, 2026

1. Introduction

Convergence Tracker ("the App") is a clinical measurement tool developed for healthcare professionals to measure ocular convergence using the iPhone's TrueDepth camera. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles data. We are committed to protecting your privacy and the privacy of any individuals whose facial data is processed by the App.

2. Data Collection and Processing

On-Device Processing Only. The App uses Apple's ARKit framework to access the TrueDepth camera for real-time face tracking. All facial landmark data, eye tracking computations, and convergence measurements are processed entirely on the device. No facial data, eye tracking data, camera images, or biometric information is transmitted to any external server, cloud service, or third party.

Camera Data. The App accesses the front-facing TrueDepth camera and the rear-facing camera. Camera data is used exclusively for real-time face tracking and eye observation within the App. No images or video are recorded, stored, or transmitted unless the user explicitly exports measurement data via the in-app export function.

Measurement Data. Convergence measurements (numerical values including convergence index, nasal shift values, vergence angles, and timestamps) are stored temporarily in the device's memory during the app session. This data is not persisted to disk unless the user explicitly chooses to export it as a CSV file using the device's standard share functionality.

3. Data the App Does NOT Collect

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any of the following: personal identification information (names, email addresses, phone numbers); facial images or photographs; biometric identifiers or templates; location data; device identifiers or advertising identifiers; usage analytics or behavioral data; health records or medical history; or any data to third-party services, advertisers, or analytics providers.

4. Face Tracking Disclosure

The App uses Apple's ARKit TrueDepth face tracking technology to detect facial landmarks and measure eye movements. In accordance with Apple's App Store Review Guidelines (Section 5.1.2), we disclose the following: the TrueDepth camera data is used solely for the purpose of measuring ocular convergence within the App. Face tracking data is not stored beyond the active session. Face tracking data is never shared with third parties. Face tracking data is not used for advertising, user profiling, or any purpose other than the clinical measurement functionality described in this policy.

5. Data Export

The App provides an optional CSV export feature that allows the user to save numerical measurement data (convergence readings, timestamps, and computed metrics). This export contains no facial images, no biometric data, and no personally identifiable information. The exported file is shared using the standard iOS share sheet, and the destination (email, AirDrop, Files, etc.) is entirely under the user's control. The developer has no access to exported data.

6. Data Retention

The App does not persist any data to the device's storage. All measurement data exists only in volatile memory during the active app session and is automatically discarded when the App is closed or terminated. There is no data to delete because no data is retained.

7. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics frameworks, advertising networks, crash reporting services, or cloud services. The App operates entirely offline and does not make any network requests.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is a clinical tool intended for use by qualified healthcare professionals. It is not directed at children under the age of 13. When the App is used by a healthcare professional to assess a minor patient, the same privacy protections described in this policy apply — no facial data or personal information is collected, stored, or transmitted.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document. Continued use of the App following any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
